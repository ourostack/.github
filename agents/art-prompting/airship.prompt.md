# Airship: ouroboros.bot home hero

## Story
Ari's agents sail his glass-and-brass airship above the clouds at sunrise while he, faceless, looks out from the bow with his pet snake Ouro beside him.

## Camera
35 mm lens. Eye level: just above the person's head, as if we stand on the stern step behind them. We look across the open deck toward the curved bow on the right. One vanishing point, on the horizon just right of centre, where the deck planks, the rail and the envelope's ribs all converge.

## Composition
Generated at 1536x1024 and shipped as a 3:1 strip, so the crop band is rows 256 to 768: every head, mitten and foot sits inside it. Headline safe area: the upper-left third of the crop band stays calm envelope glass and sky, with no figure and no bright sparkle. The cast runs from the centre to the right third; open sky, peaks and the low sun fill the right edge.

## Setting
A glass-and-brass airship gondola with an open oak deck, enclosed on every side by a brass rail with posts and teal-glass lattice panels, sailing above a sea of clouds with snowy peaks. The wind blows from the bow, so everything soft streams back toward the stern (left of frame).

## Light
Low golden sunrise from the right is the key light on every figure, with warm rim light on their right edges and a soft contact shadow under every foot and under Ouro. Ouro's inner glow spills green onto the brass rail. Sparkle is welcome on the envelope glass and the clouds.

## Palette
Sunrise peach and blue sky, cloud white, brass gold and warm oak; emerald for Ouro and the person's glow; jade, amber and sea-blue for the three agents; one red cap.

## Must not
- No third arm, no extra or missing limbs and no fused legs.
- No fingers, no pointing and no thumbs-up: mittens only.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos or symbols.
- No glowing trails or lines of light.
- No electronics of any age: brass instruments only.
- No tiles, cells or facets on the glass bodies.
- No two agents alike in colour, outfit or expression.
- No figure facing the camera full on.
- No image fades or vignettes.
- Nothing important in the top or bottom quarter of the frame.

## Output
Three candidates, each generated whole at 1536x1024, quality high. The chosen one ships as a 3072x1024 master (outpainted on both sides), a 1536x512 crop of rows 256 to 768 and a 5:3 phone crop centred on the cast. The night twin is a time-of-day edit of the chosen candidate.
