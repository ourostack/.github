# The agent's own door: Ari's LinkedIn banner

## Story
A small glass agent walks away from us through a little door built just for it, beside a tall door built for people, while its person holds the little door open.

## Camera
50 mm lens. Eye level: the camera sits at the Stoker's head height, straight on to the wall. One vanishing point, at the centre of the small doorway, so the wall, both door frames and the floor reflections stay square.

## Composition
Generated at 1536x1024; compose.py builds the 4:1 banner from it, so the crop band is rows 312 to 712: every head, foot and door top sits inside it. Safe areas: the left 45% of the banner stays calm navy stage for the line and the Microsoft and CIT logos, which are composited afterwards, and the profile photo covers the bottom-left quarter. The wall, both doors and both figures sit in the right 55%.

## Cast

### The Captain
- What: the person, CIT's Captain.
- Material: smooth, seamless frosted glass with a soft warm amber glow in the head and chest; no face.
- Build: a chunky designer-vinyl figurine: a big round head about a third of the body, a soft round torso, short stubby limbs.
- Height: about 1.4 times the Stoker's height; the top of the Stoker's head is level with the Captain's shoulder.
- Outfit: a plain amber canvas apron with no texture or pattern: the host getting the house ready for a new guest.
- Expression: faceless; a warm welcome shown by the body: head tilted toward the Stoker, weight on one hip, shoulders loose.
- Pose: standing just right of the small door's open leaf, holding it open.
- Limbs: exactly two arms, each from its own shoulder, and two legs.
- Hands: round frosted-glass mittens like winter mittens, each with one small thumb. Near (left) mitten: holds the open leaf's free edge just below its knob. Far (right) mitten: rests on the apron at the hip.
- Facing: body three-quarters toward the camera, turned toward the small door; head tilted to watch the Stoker go in.

### The Stoker
- What: the agent, CIT's Stoker, a small clear-glass robot.
- Material: smooth, seamless clear glass with a teal glow in the chest, a dark navy visor with two round teal eyes and a short antenna with a teal bead.
- Height: the reference height; the top of its antenna bead is a hand's height below the small door's arch.
- Outfit: none; the Stoker is the bare machine (CIT canon).
- Expression: eager, shown by its body: a springy mid-stride and the antenna bead glowing brighter. Its visor is turned away from us.
- Pose: mid-stride through the small doorway, the front foot on the threshold and the back foot on the stage.
- Limbs: exactly two arms and two legs, the legs clearly separate.
- Hands: both mittens swing empty at its sides.
- Facing: back to the camera, walking away from us into the warm light of its door.

## Props
- The people-door: a tall frosted-glass door, closed, in its own frame on the left of the wall. Three hinges sit in a line on its left jamb, and a round brass knob sits on its right edge at the Captain's hand height, above the Stoker's reach.
- The agent's door: a small dome-arched doorway in the same wall, right of the tall door, sized for the Stoker, its arch echoing the Stoker's helmet. A teal-glass leaf with the same dome top hangs on two hinges on the arch's right jamb and is swung open toward the camera to about 70 degrees. A round teal knob sized for a mitten sits on the leaf's free edge. Warm amber light from inside spills across the threshold; nothing shows through the arch but that light.
- The wall: one freestanding frosted-glass wall panel holding both doors, with a plain base that meets the floor in a straight line.

## Setting
CIT's house stage: a glossy deep navy-to-indigo glass floor with soft bokeh behind, and nothing else on the stage.

## Light
The warm amber spill from the small door is the key light on both figures and the floor; a soft violet-blue rim light comes from behind, and both figures reflect in the floor.

## Palette
Deep navy, indigo, frosted white glass, teal and amber.

## Must not
- No neon ring, portal, porthole, vault or round hatch door.
- No fingers, no second thumb and no pointing: mittens with one thumb.
- No face on the Captain and no mouth on the Stoker.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos or symbols.
- No trails, streaks or lines of light.
- No room, furniture or scenery visible through the small door.
- No texture, stitching or pattern on the apron.
- No device of any kind.
- No speckle, crackle or sharpening halos.
- Nothing important in the left 45% or the bottom-left quarter.

## Output
Three candidates, each generated whole at 1536x1024, quality high. compose.py places the chosen master's figures at no more than 1.2x on a 3840x960 stage and downsizes it to 3168x792 and 1584x396; the figures are never AI-upscaled.
