# Ouro art principles

The rule itself is [agents/art-one-universe.md](../../agents/art-one-universe.md): one universe, different galaxies, one bar. This page only holds what is Ouro's galaxy and how its art is made; it does not restate the rule.

Why the best product art (Apple keynote scenes, Stripe and Linear 3D art, Pixar and studio concept art) reads as serious and delightful at once, and what Ouro takes from it:

1. People are archetypes, not portraits: a smooth, faceless figure, filled with inner light.
2. One material language per world. Everything, mascot included, is glass, brass and oak.
3. One focal action, with depth: foreground, middle, far. Subjects sit in the middle band.
4. Real physics: a ground plane, correct scale, hands on real handles, nothing floating or unexplained.
5. Restraint: a small palette, one light source, calm backgrounds. Delight comes from one surprising detail (the snake, lit through), not from clutter.
6. Wit lives in the craft, so Ouro's eyes are glass and gems in the same material as his body, not painted cartoon eyes.
7. Same universe, different galaxies: Ouro's galaxy changes colour, inner light, accessory and place, never the material.
8. Compose each frame for the aspect it ships at (heroes about 3:1): all three characters in the safe area, headline space upper left, and legible at about 400 px wide.
