# Empty week: the calendar Moment's spot art

## Story
Ouro lies curled in the open middle of a calm frosted-glass week with nothing booked, looking up at us, content and unhurried.

## Camera
50 mm lens, premium animated-film render, natural film colour. Looking at the glass week from slightly above and in front, three-quarters on, at eye level with Ouro's head, a little above the glass week. One vanishing point, at the centre of the glass week's back edge.

## Composition
Generated at 1024x1024 on one perfectly flat, uniform, pure bright magenta background (#FF00FF) with no gradient, no shadow, no floor and nothing else on it, and shipped whole as a keyed square sprite. The whole group (the glass week and Ouro) fits inside the middle 80 percent of the frame, with an even margin on every side; the safe area is that middle 80 percent. The glass week is a wide, low object centred a little below the middle of the frame; Ouro lies in the centre of it. The crop band is the whole square: nothing important is cut, and nothing touches the frame's edge.

## Cast

### Ouro
- What: Ouro, the pet snake, exactly as in the second reference image.
- Material: smooth, seamless glossy emerald body with a gentle inner glow and a pale jade belly; the face, eyes and smile of the second reference image, with eyes of glass and gems in the same material as his body.
- Height: his whole coil is about as tall as one of the glass week's day slabs is wide, and fills about a third of the glass week's width.
- Outfit: none.
- Expression: calm and content, eyes wide and relaxed, a gentle smile.
- Body: an open "C" curl, never a closed ring, donut or torus. The head and the short tail tip are the two ends of one body, with a visible gap between them where the body never meets itself.
- Pose: resting on the glass week's centre, his head lifted a little toward the camera and his one short tail tip rising at the other end of the open curl, just to the right of his head.
- Limbs: none: one head, one continuous body, one short tail tip.
- Hands: none.
- Facing: head in three-quarter view toward the camera, so we see both eyes and his smile.

## Props
- The glass week: one low, wide slab of smooth, thick, milky frosted glass in the shape of a calendar week: seven equal, plain, empty rounded slots in one row, set a little into the slab, each slot as wide as it is tall and perfectly blank; a slim brushed-brass binding strip runs along the slab's back edge with three small brass rings. Warm light glows softly inside the glass. Every slot is empty: nothing in them, on them or over them.

## Setting
None: the group floats nowhere; the key colour fills everything else. No table, floor, shadow or reflection.

## Light
One soft golden-hour key light from the upper left, giving a warm glow on the frosted glass and cool emerald shadows under Ouro's coil on the glass itself. A soft inner glow in the glass.

## Palette
Milky frosted glass in cream and pale jade, brushed brass, emerald for Ouro. No magenta, pink or purple anywhere on the subject.

## Must not
- No text, letters, numbers, dots, marks, symbols or logos anywhere: the slots are blank.
- No events, tick marks, cubes, props or objects in the slots.
- No person and no robot: only Ouro and the glass week.
- No shadow, floor, table, reflection, glow or fade outside the subject; the background is flat magenta all the way to the edges.
- No closed ring: Ouro never bites or touches his own tail.
- No tiles, cells or facets on Ouro's body.
- No comic or cartoon styling: no outlines, no exaggerated squash and stretch.
- No glowing trails or lines of light.
- No magenta, pink or purple on the subject.
- No image fades or vignettes.

## Output
Three candidates, each generated whole at 1024x1024, quality high. The chosen one is keyed to transparency and shipped as a 440 px square; its dark twin is an edit of the chosen picture: the same place, the golden light replaced by cool moonlight, the glass glowing a soft emerald from within, everything else the same.
